# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free community. Examples of positive behavior include:

- communicating respectfully and constructively;
- focusing criticism on code and technical decisions;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- helping newcomers understand the project;
- respecting differing experience, backgrounds and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other public spaces used to represent this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition and the safety of the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
